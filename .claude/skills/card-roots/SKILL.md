# Writing a card's roots

On a Garden board the one guide is `~/.garden/roots/detail/composing-roots.md`: what a brief contains,
in what order, at what length and in what voice, and what it leaves out. Read it and follow it. How
to make the card, the commands and their order, is the `hiring-a-card` skill.

This skill used to carry its own guidance. It disagreed with the guide in several places, asking for
the standards a card inherits, how a report is sent, and a story behind every trap, and the briefs
that followed it drifted from the files they copied. It is a pointer so that there is one source.
